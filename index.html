The Practitioner

Dr. Tabrez Khan
MBBS, PGDADC (Diabetes Care)

Medicine is not merely about prescriptions; it is about partnership. Since commencing practice in 2023, Dr. Khan has dedicated himself to a philosophy where the patient's voice is the most critical diagnostic tool.

A graduate of Al-Ameen Medical College, Dr. Khan further specialized with a Post Graduate Diploma in Advanced Diabetes Care. This unique blend of general medical expertise and specialized chronic disease management allows Dr. Khan's Clinic to offer a holistic roadmap to wellness.

Whether treating acute seasonal ailments or managing complex lifestyle disorders, the approach remains constant: Empathy, Evidence, and Excellence.
